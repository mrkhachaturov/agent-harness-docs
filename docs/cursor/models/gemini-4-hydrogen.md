Gemini 4 Hydrogen is Google's fast, cost-efficient Gemini model and the successor to [Gemini 3.8 Flash](https://cursor.com/docs/models/gemini-3-8-flash.md). Use it for everyday agentic coding and tool-heavy work. For the hardest tasks, use [Gemini 4 Carbon](https://cursor.com/docs/models/gemini-4-carbon.md).

## Effort levels

Gemini 4 Hydrogen supports four effort levels: xhigh, high (the default), medium, and low. Keep it on high for agentic coding. Quality drops noticeably at medium and low, so use those only for simple, latency-sensitive tasks. Raise it to xhigh for hard debugging and reasoning, at the cost of more thinking tokens and slower responses.

# Subscribe from the mobile app

Grok Bot mobile signs in with the same Cursor account you use on desktop. If your Cursor account already has Grok Bot access, that access carries over to mobile without a second purchase.

## Which plans carry over to mobile?

Sign in to Grok Bot mobile with the **same Cursor account** you use on desktop, and access carries over for:

- **Cursor Ultra**
- **Cursor Pro+**
- **Cursor Pro**
- **Cursor Teams** (self-serve) seats
- **SuperGrok**, **SuperGrok Plus**, or **SuperGrok Heavy** account links
- **X Premium+** account links

You should not buy a second subscription on Apple or Google Play in these cases.

## How do I subscribe to Grok Bot from the mobile app?

If your Cursor account doesn't already include Grok Bot, subscribe from the mobile app:

1. Open Grok Bot on your phone.
2. Choose a plan on the paywall screen.
3. Complete the in-app purchase through the App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android.

Only monthly individual plans are available through the mobile app. For yearly billing, Teams, or Enterprise plans, subscribe on [cursor.com](https://cursor.com/dashboard/billing). See [Subscribe from the iOS app](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/app-store-subscription.md) for how Apple handles in-app subscriptions, or [Subscribe from the Android app](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/google-play-subscription.md) for how Google Play handles them.

## How do I use SuperGrok or X Premium+ on mobile?

You still need to [link your Grok or X account](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/supergrok.md). Complete the SuperGrok or X Premium+ link on desktop, then sign in to Grok Bot mobile with the same Cursor account.

## How do I manage or cancel a mobile subscription?

The store you bought through handles the subscription. Cursor can't cancel an in-app plan for you.

- **iOS:** Apple handles in-app subscriptions. Cancel or change the plan in **Apple ID → Subscriptions**. For Apple billing questions, use Apple's subscription support.
- **Android:** Google Play handles in-app subscriptions. Cancel or change the plan in the Google Play app under **Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**. For Google Play billing questions, use Google Play's subscription support.

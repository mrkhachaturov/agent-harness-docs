# Pages

A page is something a Bot builds for you to read, such as a report, a memo, a dashboard, a tracker, or an explainer. It can hold text, tables, charts, and controls like filters and switches. A page lives in the Bot's Library, so you can come back to it later and share it.

Pages is rolling out gradually, and it isn't available on Enterprise teams yet. If you don't see pages, update Grok Bot to version 0.60.0 or later on your computer and 1.14.0 or later on your phone.

## How do I make a page?

Ask the Bot in chat. Say what the page is for and what should be on it. For example: "Make a page that compares the three quotes in this thread, with a table and a short recommendation."

The Bot builds the page and posts it in the chat as a card with the page's title and **Open**.

## How do I open a page?

On desktop, click the page card in the chat. The page fills the window, and the chat stays open in a smaller panel beside it, so you can keep talking to the Bot while you read.

On the phone, tap the page card in the chat to open it in the app.

When the Bot updates a page, the earlier card in the chat says **Updated below**. Clicking it opens the latest version.

## How do I change a page?

Ask the Bot. While the page is open, type in the chat panel beside it, labeled **Message about** and the page's title. The Bot edits the same page, so everyone who can open it sees the change.

Filters, switches, and other controls on a page change what you see, not what the page holds. Nothing you do on the page is saved. To change the page itself, ask the Bot.

To rename a page you made, click its title at the top of the open page.

## Where do I find a Bot's pages?

On desktop:

1. Click the Bot's name at the top of the chat to open its details.
2. Open the **Library** tab.

The Library lists the pages, files, and apps the Bot has made. A page shared with your team or with anyone who has the link shows that on its row.

Click the **More actions** button (the three dots) on a page's row to see **Open**, **Share…**, and **Pin**. Pinned pages stay at the top of the Library, under **Pinned**. Choose **Unpin** to take a page off the list.

## How do I share a page?

A new page is private to you until you share it.

Open the page and click **Share** at the top. You can also choose **Share…** from the page's menu in the Library.

Under **Who can view**, choose one of these:

| Option                             | Who can open the page                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **People you invite**              | You and the teammates and groups you add. Each one gets **Viewer** access. On a personal account, this option is **Only you**. |
| **Your team**, or your team's name | Everyone on your Cursor team.                                                                                                  |
| **Anyone with the link**           | Anyone, with no sign-in needed.                                                                                                |

Some teams also see an option for everyone in their organization.

After you choose, click **Copy link** and send the link.

People who open a team or invite link sign in with their Cursor account first. Everyone you share with can read the page and use its controls. Only the Bot can change it.

To share a page with fewer people, open **Share** again and choose a narrower option. You can also change who can view it from your dashboard. See [Where do I see every page I've shared?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/pages.md#where-do-i-see-every-page-ive-shared)

## What happens when I share a page with anyone who has the link?

The page gets its own web address on `pages.grok.bot`, made from the page's title. Anyone with the address can open the page without signing in, so share it only when the page holds nothing private.

The address stays the same if you rename the page later. You can't choose a custom address yet.

Public sharing is off on Enterprise teams. If you have a personal account, or you're on a team that isn't on the Enterprise plan, you can share a page with anyone who has the link.

## Where do I see every page I've shared?

Go to [Shared Canvases](https://cursor.com/dashboard/shared-canvases) on your Cursor dashboard. Pages you've shared are listed under **Grok Bot Pages**. From there you can change who can view a page, or delete it. Deleting a page means anyone with the link loses access.

## Why can't I share a page the way I want?

The share menu says why when an option isn't available.

| What you see                                            | What it means                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Public sharing is off for Enterprise teams**          | Your team is on the Enterprise plan. Share the page with your team or with people you invite instead.             |
| **Pages in your personal space can't be team-shared**   | The page belongs to your personal account, not a team. Keep it private, or share it with anyone who has the link. |
| **Couldn't update access**                              | The change didn't save. Try again.                                                                                |
| **This page's access changed. Make your change again.** | Someone changed who can view the page while you had the menu open. Make your change again.                        |

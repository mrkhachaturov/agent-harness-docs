# Get access with SuperGrok Heavy

Link your Grok account to unlock free Cursor Ultra if you subscribe to SuperGrok Heavy.

## What do I need before linking SuperGrok Heavy?

You need an active **SuperGrok Heavy** subscription on the Grok account you will link, and a Cursor or Grok Bot account to receive access.

Linking pairs one Cursor account with one Grok account for the promotion. The link itself is not a Cursor payment.

## What do I get after linking SuperGrok Heavy?

Qualifying customers receive **free Cursor Ultra** created at $0, which includes Grok Bot access. The subscription remains active at no charge so long as the SuperGrok Heavy plan is active on renewal.

No Cursor payment card is required.

## How do I link my Grok account?

1. On the Grok Bot plan screen, choose **Get access with SuperGrok Heavy**.
2. Click **Link Grok Account**.
3. Sign into the **Grok account that has SuperGrok Heavy**.
4. Return to Grok Bot and confirm you have access.

## How does the SuperGrok Heavy offer affect Cursor Pro or Pro+?

**Read this before you claim:** if you have a Stripe-billed Cursor Pro or Pro+ subscription and claim free Ultra through SuperGrok Heavy, Cursor cancels your current Pro or Pro+ plan and replaces it with free Ultra.

If the free Ultra later ends, your old Pro or Pro+ plan does **not** return automatically.

Apple-billed Pro or Pro+ subscriptions cannot be transitioned automatically, so the promotion may be declined rather than stacking Ultra on top.

## Which accounts are not eligible for the SuperGrok Heavy offer?

- **Already on Ultra:** linking SuperGrok Heavy does not add extra Ultra access. You already have Grok Bot through Ultra.
- **Team accounts:** the individual SuperGrok promotion is not eligible. Your admin manages Grok Bot access with team seats.
- **Other Grok plans:** this promotion requires SuperGrok Heavy. Choose another access option available to your account. See [Plans and billing](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans.md).
- **One Grok account to one Cursor account:** a Grok account can't use this promotion on two Cursor accounts at once.

## Why am I still on the plan screen after linking?

Confirm you're signed in with the same Cursor account. If access still does not appear, [contact support](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/get-help.md).

## Why don't I see a Link Grok Account option after I've already logged in?

The **Link Grok Account** option appears on the **Get Started** paywall screen before you sign in. Once you are past the paywall and inside the main app, Settings does not show a Grok or xAI account link option.

To return to the paywall screen:

1. Fully quit Grok Bot (on Mac, choose **Quit** from the menu bar).
2. Relaunch the app.

If you still do not see **Link Grok Account**, confirm you are not already on Ultra through another path. See [Which accounts are not eligible for the SuperGrok Heavy offer?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/supergrok-heavy.md#which-accounts-are-not-eligible-for-the-supergrok-heavy-offer).

## Why does my Ultra from SuperGrok Heavy show a pending cancellation date?

Ultra granted through SuperGrok Heavy is included at no charge for as long as your SuperGrok Heavy subscription stays active. Cursor re-checks Heavy status at each renewal.

A **pending cancellation date** (cancel at period end) on the grant can appear from an older provisioning path. It does not mean you will be charged $200 per month while SuperGrok Heavy remains active.

If your plan screen shows a pending cancellation date and you want it removed, [contact support](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/get-help.md) with:

- The email on your Cursor account
- The pending cancellation date shown in the app
- Confirmation that your SuperGrok Heavy subscription is still active

Support can clear the pending cancellation flag. You should not be charged $200 while SuperGrok Heavy stays active.

## What if my SuperGrok Heavy access, email, or usage looks wrong after linking?

If linking SuperGrok Heavy didn't unlock Cursor Ultra, applied to the wrong account, or your usage didn't reset, work through these checks first.

**Confirm the basics:**

- You linked the **Grok account that holds the active SuperGrok Heavy subscription**. Access is tied to that specific Grok account.
- You're signed into Grok Bot with the **same Cursor account** you linked.
- Your SuperGrok Heavy subscription is still active at renewal.

**Common failure modes:**

| What you're seeing                            | What to try                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Linking fails partway through sign-in         | Fully quit Grok Bot (on Mac, choose **Quit** from the menu bar), reopen, and link again from the **Get Started** screen.                                                                                     |
| Access applied to the wrong email             | Access follows the Grok account that has SuperGrok Heavy, so re-link using that account. A Grok account can unlock only one Cursor account at a time.                                                        |
| Usage didn't reset after upgrading            | Confirm your Ultra is active and not pending cancellation, then allow up to 24 hours after a successful link for usage to refresh.                                                                           |
| Plan screen shows a pending cancellation date | See [Why does my Ultra from SuperGrok Heavy show a pending cancellation date?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/supergrok-heavy.md#why-does-my-ultra-from-supergrok-heavy-show-a-pending-cancellation-date). |

If your access, email, or usage still looks wrong after these steps, [contact support](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/get-help.md) with the email on your Cursor account, the Grok account email you linked, and a screenshot of your plan screen.

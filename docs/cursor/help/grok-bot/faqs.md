# Grok Bot FAQs

Short answers for access, usage, and using Grok Bot. Longer steps live on the linked articles.

## Do I need a separate Grok Bot subscription?

No. Grok Bot comes with **Cursor Pro**, **Pro+**, **Ultra**, and self-serve **Teams**. You can also link an individual **SuperGrok**, **SuperGrok Plus**, **SuperGrok Heavy**, or **X Premium+** account. On Enterprise, ask your account team. See [Plans and billing](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans.md#how-do-i-get-access-to-grok-bot).

## Is Grok Bot the same as the Grok app?

No. Grok (the chat app from xAI) and Grok Bot are two different apps. A paywall or a **Weekly SuperGrok Limit** in the Grok app is not about Grok Bot. Grok Bot signs in with your Cursor account. See [Sign in to Grok Bot](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/sign-in.md).

## Which Grok plans can I link?

Individual **SuperGrok**, **SuperGrok Plus**, **SuperGrok Heavy**, and **X Premium+**. **SuperGrok Lite**, **SuperGrok Team**, and **SuperGrok Enterprise** cannot link. See [Which SuperGrok tiers grant Grok Bot?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/supergrok.md#which-supergrok-tiers-grant-grok-bot).

## I don't see Link Grok Account.

That option shows only on the **Get Started** screen, before you sign in. Settings does not have it. Fully quit Grok Bot (on Mac, choose **Quit** from the menu bar), then open it again. See [Why don't I see a Link Grok Account option after I've already logged in?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/supergrok.md#why-dont-i-see-a-link-grok-account-option-after-ive-already-logged-in).

## I linked SuperGrok to the wrong Cursor account. Can I unlink it?

No. A link is permanent, and you cannot move it yourself. [Contact support](https://cursor.com/help.md) with the account that got the link and the account that should have it. See [Can I unlink or move a SuperGrok link?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/supergrok.md#can-i-unlink-or-move-a-supergrok-link-to-a-different-cursor-account).

## Can I change the email on my account?

No. The email on a Cursor account cannot change. To use a different email, make a new account. See [Change your email address](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/change-email.md).

## I'm stuck in a sign-in loop or see an authentication error.

Update the app, then sign out and sign in again. Clear cookies for cursor.com and x.ai and try a private window. If the phone loops, sign in on desktop first. If the error is new, wait 30 to 60 minutes. Reinstalling usually does not help. See [Sign in to Grok Bot](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/sign-in.md#why-is-grok-bot-showing-a-persistent-authentication-error-even-after-i-log-out-clear-cache-and-reinstall).

## My Bot stopped replying and says I hit my usage limit. Is it broken?

No. Your weekly usage is used up. It resets each week. To keep going now, turn on on-demand usage or move to a bigger Cursor plan. See [How does Grok Bot usage work?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans.md#how-does-grok-bot-usage-work).

## Why doesn't my SuperGrok limit match my Grok Bot usage?

They are two separate meters for two products. Grok Bot usage lives on your Cursor account. See it in Grok Bot under **Settings** > **Usage & Billing**, not in the Grok app. See [What do the plan and spending screens call usage?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans.md#what-do-the-plan-and-spending-screens-call-usage).

## Do a Cursor plan and SuperGrok add together?

No. They do not stack. For more usage, upgrade your Cursor plan (for example **Pro+** to **Ultra**) or turn on on-demand usage. See [Do Cursor and SuperGrok plans stack?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans.md#do-cursor-and-supergrok-plans-stack).

## How do I stop charges after my weekly usage runs out?

Turn off on-demand, or set a cap. In Grok Bot, open **Settings** > **On-demand monthly limit**. On the web, go to [cursor.com/dashboard](https://cursor.com/dashboard) > **Spending** > **Monthly Limit**. The cap does not stop a Bot in the middle of a run, so a running task can go a little past it. See [How does the on-demand monthly limit apply to Grok Bot?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans.md#how-does-the-on-demand-monthly-limit-apply-to-grok-bot) and [Usage-based charges](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/overages.md).

## My trial ran out in one task. Can I get it back?

No. The trial is a usage credit, and one big task can use all of it. Used credit is not restored. See [What if my trial ran out during a single run?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans.md#what-if-my-trial-ran-out-during-a-single-run).

## I paid in the iPhone or Android app. How do I cancel or get a refund?

Apple or Google handles it. Cancel in **Apple ID** > **Subscriptions**, or in **Google Play** > **Payments & subscriptions**. Cursor cannot cancel or refund store purchases. See [Subscribe from the iOS app](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/app-store-subscription.md#how-do-i-manage-an-in-app-subscription), [Subscribe from the Android app](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/google-play-subscription.md#how-do-i-manage-an-in-app-subscription), and [Refunds](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/refunds.md).

## Do I have to pay again on my phone?

No. Sign in to the phone app with the same Cursor account, and your access carries over. See [Do I need to buy again on mobile?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/mobile.md#do-i-need-to-buy-again-on-mobile-if-my-cursor-account-already-includes-grok-bot).

## Does my Bot keep working when my laptop is closed?

Yes. Bots work on a cloud computer. Closing the app, the laptop, or the phone does not stop a task or a routine. See [Routines](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/routines.md).

## Does the Bot use my own computer?

Bots work on their own cloud computer. A Bot runs a command on your desktop only if you allow it under **Settings** > **Execution on Local Computer**. By default it asks you each time. See [Grok Bot settings](https://cursor.com/docs/grok-bot/settings.md).

## Why does a website block my Bot or show a captcha?

Some sites block traffic from cloud computers. When that happens, the Bot hands you the step, and you can sign in yourself on the Bot's computer. You can also turn on **Settings** > **Route egress through this desktop**, so sites see your own connection.

## My chats or Bots are missing.

They are usually not deleted. Fully quit and reopen. Check another device on the same account. Open **Hidden Bots**. Make sure you are on the same Cursor account. Do not use **Reset** to look for them. See [My chats or bots look missing](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/computer-recovery.md#my-chats-or-bots-look-missing-were-they-deleted).

## The computer says Reconnecting or "Couldn't Reach Grok Bot's Computer."

Wait and choose **Retry**. Then fully quit and reopen. Then **Update**, then **Recover**. Use **Reset** only last, because it can lose recent unsynced work. See [Which should I try first?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/computer-recovery.md#which-should-i-try-first).

## Can I pick the model?

No. Cursor picks the model for the specific task under the hood. There is no model picker in Grok Bot. See [Grok Bot settings](https://cursor.com/docs/grok-bot/settings.md).

## Which devices work?

The desktop app runs on Mac, Windows, and Linux. The phone app runs on iPhone and Android. Get the desktop app from the Grok Bot page at [cursor.com/dashboard/bot](https://cursor.com/dashboard/bot). See [Grok Bot on mobile](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/mobile.md).

## How do I contact support?

[Contact support](https://cursor.com/help.md) while signed in to your Cursor account. Include your account email, your Grok Bot version (account menu > **About** > **Copy version info**), a screenshot, and the request ID (right-click the message > **Copy request ID**). See [How do I find my Grok Bot version?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/how-to.md#how-do-i-find-my-grok-bot-version) and [How do I copy a request ID?](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/how-to.md#how-do-i-copy-a-request-id).

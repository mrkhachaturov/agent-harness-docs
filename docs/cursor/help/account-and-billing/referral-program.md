# Referral program

Cursor discontinued the referral program for Cursor plans. Grok Bot on The X Subscription has its own, separate referral program. See [Does Grok Bot have a referral program?](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/referral-program.md#does-grok-bot-have-a-referral-program) below.

This page covers the discontinued referral program for Cursor plans. If you came here from the gift icon or **Invite** button in Grok Bot, that is the Grok Bot referral program, which is active and is described at the end of this page.

## Why did Cursor discontinue the referral program?

Cursor ended the referral program due to increased levels of fraud. New referral invites and referral rewards are no longer available.

## What happens to referral credits I already earned?

If you already received referral credits, they remain on your account. You can apply them to eligible charges the same way as before. You can't earn new referral rewards or send new referral invites through the program.

## Can I still use a referral link I saved?

Referral links from the program no longer grant discounts or referral rewards for new sign-ups

## How can I get a discount on Cursor now?

You still have other ways to save on Cursor:

- Compare plans on [Pricing and plans](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/pricing.md). Yearly plans offer a 20% discount.
- If you're a student, see [Student discount](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/student-discount.md) for current campus events, credits, and newsletter sign-up options.

## Where can I get help with referral credits on my account?

Open [cursor.com/dashboard/billing](https://cursor.com/dashboard/billing) to review your subscription and billing history. For referral credit balance questions, [contact support](https://cursor.com/help.md) while signed in and include your account email and a short description of the issue.

## Does Grok Bot have a referral program?

Yes. Grok Bot on [The X Subscription](https://cursor.com/help/the-x-subscription/overview.md) has its own referral program. It's separate from the discontinued Cursor program above, and it's rolling out gradually, so it isn't on every eligible account yet.

**Who can refer.** You need a paid individual **Premium**, **Super**, or **Ultra** plan on The X Subscription, billed in the US, bought on the web rather than through the App Store or Google Play, and at least 7 days after checkout. Plus, team seats, and legacy Cursor or SuperGrok plans can't refer.

**Where to find it.** When it's on for your account, look for the gift icon: at the bottom left of the Grok Bot desktop app, at the top right of a chat in the phone app (version 1.18.0 or later; some accounts see the word **Invite** instead of the icon), and under **Referrals** at [grok.com/dashboard](https://grok.com/dashboard). Choose **Share Link** to copy your link. If you meet the requirements and don't see it yet, there's nothing to turn on. It appears on its own as the rollout continues.

**What your friend gets.** A friend who signs up through your link gets their first 2 weeks of Grok Bot free.

**What you get.** A usage reset is recorded on your account 7 days after your friend's first payment, and it stays valid for 6 months. The ability to use these resets is switching on gradually. Once it's enabled for your account, the reset shows in Grok Bot under **Settings** > **Usage** as a **Usage Limit Reset**, and on the dashboard under **Usage resets**. The gift icon screen shows how many friends have redeemed your code, counting friends still on their trial or inside the 7 days after their first payment. That number can take up to a day to update; opening the screen refreshes it. If your friend's payment is refunded or disputed, the reset is removed.

**If you see "This offer isn't available on your account."** Your account doesn't meet the requirements above, most often because the plan is billed through an app store, isn't an individual Premium, Super, or Ultra plan, or is less than 7 days old.

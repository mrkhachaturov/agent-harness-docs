# Billing and payments

Understand your billing cycle, renewals, payment methods, and invoices.

## How does my billing cycle work?

Your cycle starts on the date you subscribe. It renews monthly or yearly based on your plan.

- Individual plans renew on your signup date
- Teams plans renew on the team signup date
- Renewal dates stay the same when members are added or removed

## When does included usage reset?

Included usage resets with your billing cycle.

- Individual plans reset monthly
- Teams plans include usage per paid seat each cycle, based on [seat type](https://cursor.com/docs/account/teams/members.md#seat-types) (Standard or Premium)
- Usage beyond included amounts appears as on-demand usage

## What can create charges before renewal?

You can see charges between renewal dates when one of these events happens:

- On-demand usage is billed after included usage is consumed
- Team seats are added mid-cycle and charged pro-rata
- Team seat removals can create billing adjustments or credits

## How do seats work on a Teams plan?

Cursor bills teams per active paid seat, not pre-allocated seats. Seat price depends on the seat type (Standard or Premium).

- Adding a member mid-cycle creates a pro-rated charge
- Removing a member who used credits keeps the seat occupied until the cycle ends
- Billing adjustments appear on a future invoice as credit when applicable

## How do I update my payment method?

**Billed on cursor.com:**

1. Go to [cursor.com/dashboard/billing](https://cursor.com/dashboard/billing)
2. Click **Manage in Stripe** to open the Stripe billing portal
3. Update your card in the Stripe billing portal

The billing page does not show **Manage in Stripe** for an App Store plan or a plan bought in the Grok Bot Android app.

**Billed through the App Store:**

Apple manages the payment method. Update the card in your Apple ID subscriptions. See [Subscribe from the iOS app](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/app-store-subscription.md#how-do-i-manage-an-in-app-subscription).

**Billed in the Grok Bot Android app:**

Google Play manages the payment method. Manage the card in the Google Play app. See [Subscribe from the Android app](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/google-play-subscription.md#how-do-i-manage-an-in-app-subscription).

On an App Store plan, or a plan bought in the Grok Bot Android app, on-demand usage uses a separate card on the web. Add or change it under **Spending** on [cursor.com/dashboard](https://cursor.com/dashboard). See [On-demand usage for mobile subscriptions](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/on-demand-usage-for-mobile-subscriptions.md).

## How do I update billing details for invoices?

For a plan billed on cursor.com, update your company name, address, and tax details in the Stripe billing portal. Changes apply to future invoices. An App Store receipt comes from Apple. A receipt for a plan bought in the Grok Bot Android app comes from Google Play. See [Subscribe from the iOS app](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/app-store-subscription.md#how-do-i-manage-an-in-app-subscription) or [Subscribe from the Android app](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/google-play-subscription.md#how-do-i-manage-an-in-app-subscription).

## Where do I find invoices and receipts?

**Billed on cursor.com:**

Go to [cursor.com/dashboard/billing](https://cursor.com/dashboard/billing) and click **Manage in Stripe**. In the Stripe portal, open any invoice to view or download the PDF. See [Invoices](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/invoices.md) for details.

**Billed through the App Store:**

Apple sends the receipt for the subscription. See [Subscribe from the iOS app](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/app-store-subscription.md#how-do-i-manage-an-in-app-subscription).

**Billed in the Grok Bot Android app:**

Google Play bills this subscription, and Google sends the receipt. See [Subscribe from the Android app](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/google-play-subscription.md#how-do-i-manage-an-in-app-subscription).

On-demand usage on either plan is billed to the separate card on your dashboard, so those invoices are not store receipts. Open [cursor.com/dashboard/billing](https://cursor.com/dashboard/billing) and scroll to **Invoices**.

## What if I still need billing help?

[Contact support](https://cursor.com/help.md) while signed in to your Cursor account and include invoice IDs, dates, and a short issue description.

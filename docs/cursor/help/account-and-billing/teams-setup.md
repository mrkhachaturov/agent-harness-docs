# Set up a team

Create a team account so your organization can share a Cursor subscription with centralized billing and admin controls.

## How do I create a team?

1. Go to [cursor.com/dashboard](https://cursor.com/dashboard)
2. Click **Create Team**
3. Name your team and select a billing plan
4. Complete the payment

## How do I invite members?

You can invite members by email or by sharing an invite link.

### Invite by email

Open your team dashboard:

- Click **Members** > **Invite** to open the **Invite members** popup
- Enter email addresses
- Choose a role: **Admin** or **Member**
- Click **Send invites**

Members receive an email with a link to join your team.

### Invite with a link

Share a team invite link with anyone you want to join. By default, anyone with the link can join until it expires.

Admins can manage invite links in your team settings under **Team invite links**:

- **Allow members to invite teammates**: When off, only admins can invite members or create invite links.
- **Require approval to join with a link**: When on, people who open an invite link ask to join, and an admin approves or denies them. Email invites still join directly.
- **Default expiration**: Sets the longest lifetime for new invite links. Lowering it also shortens existing links.
- **Active invite links**: Click **Manage** to see your team's links and revoke any of them.

## How do I approve requests to join my team?

If your team requires approval to join with a link, new requests show up on the **Members** page.

1. Open your team dashboard and go to **Members**
2. Select the **Requests** tab
3. Open the menu next to the requester's name
4. Choose to approve or deny the request

Admins receive an email when someone asks to join, and the requester receives an email once their request is approved or denied.

To let people join right away, turn off **Require approval to join with a link**.

## What's the difference between admin and member roles?

- **Admins** can manage billing, invite/remove members, configure SSO, and view analytics
- **Unpaid Admins** can manage the team without using a paid seat. They have the same admin permissions but don't count toward your seat total.
- **Members** can use Cursor with the team subscription but can't manage team settings
